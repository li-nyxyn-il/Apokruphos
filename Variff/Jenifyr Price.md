# Jenifyr Price

## Description

Twin sister of [[Demien Price]] and daughter of [[Jhyn Price]] and [[Maiahna Ihryl]], born 531. She has ghostly skin, auburn hair and the blood eyes of her Line, which she once took for "a mark of purity" and now knows to be a curse. She worshipped [[Ilys]] as a girl. At the Penhyla summit's masquerade she danced with a masked southern stranger, [[Halion Dane]], and she is now with child by him.

After her mother and brother die, Jenifyr flees with her father through Ptianhyliv. Her uncle [[Vanar Price]] catches her on a ship, where she is beaten and assaulted until Jhyn returns for her. The khronologies list her as dead "of unknown causes", and her body was never found. Her chapters follow the search for "some secret place" and the hidden valley, and the meeting with her uncle [[Ihlven Ihryl]] across the lake.
