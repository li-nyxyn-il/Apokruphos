# Mehania Dane

## Description

Daughter of [[Vakirros Grymsyhl]] and [[Mehni Dane]], named Mehania Grymsyhl by her father but Mehania Dane by the conquered, under the Dyikhana custom that a secondary wife's daughter belongs to her mother's house. She bore nine children to nine men across the continent; they became the first Prelates of the Nine Beyonds. Her last child, by a [[Nyxians|Nyxian]] noble, was stillborn. She named the child [[Ythinyx]] and vanished north with her (see [[Ancient Lore]]).
