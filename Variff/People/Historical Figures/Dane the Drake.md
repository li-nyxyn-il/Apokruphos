# Dane the Drake

## Description

Founder of [[Line of Dane]], later deified as [[Drake]]. In [[Ancient Lore]] he is a Cold Flame of [[The Phionyx]], banished by the other Flame Priests. He gained power in the mountains beyond [[Shentzlia]], returned with an army down the rivers, and founded the Draciian order, which ruled the Shentzli valley for a thousand years.
