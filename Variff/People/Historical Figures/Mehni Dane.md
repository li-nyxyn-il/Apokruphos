# Mehni Dane

## Description

The Shentzli Draci at the time of the Yhlgori conquest; her name translates to "above all". [[Vakirros Grymsyhl]] claimed her as a prize-wife in the belief that her child would be the Mehania he sought (see [[Ancient Lore]]).
