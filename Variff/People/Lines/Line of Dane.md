# Line of Dane

A Shentzlian Line ("Dane is a Shentzlian Line", Hesseh tells Aiden) of the [[Shentzi]], founded by [[Dane the Drake]] and descended through [[Mehania Dane]]. [[Alynare Dane]] heads it from [[Maphlamar]], where it shares the estate with the Audsens. Her children carry the Dane name, not their father's.
