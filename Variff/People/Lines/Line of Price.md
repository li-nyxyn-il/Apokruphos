# Line of Price

A Ptormyk Line of the Atzh'ptorm, one of the Lines of Ptormia. Its Patron, [[Williem Price]], governs the Atzh'ptorm. Like many Ptormyk Lines, its khronologies show firstborn heirs dying in infancy, their names passed to the next son. Aiden finds that it descends in part from Sigres Dane.
