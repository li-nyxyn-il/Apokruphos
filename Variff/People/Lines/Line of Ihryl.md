# Line of Ihryl

The Pynumbral Line of Ptormia, seated at Vinhrest. With [[Ztiodyn Ihryl]] dead and his heir an infant, the Line is "shattered": the regency belongs to the Ptorhan and [[Ihlven Ihryl]] is in exile.
