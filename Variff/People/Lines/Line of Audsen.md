# Line of Audsen

The Line of the [[Grymsol Elohaim]] and the Umbral Throne, dominant in Aphlora and the Pynumbra of Hale. Over the past century the other major Lines of Hale died off, were subsumed by it, or moved west. Generations of Audsen heirs married into the Aulden, Skyf, Dwyte and Field Lines. [[Havil Audsen]] governs [[Phorva]] from [[Maphlamar]].
