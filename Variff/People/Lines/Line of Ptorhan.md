# Line of Ptorhan

One of the Lines of Ptormia. Hylvaer Fuhlen Ptorhan now governs Ptian from Ptianhyliv, and his daughter [[Gweine Ptorhan]] is regent for the infant Grymsol Pynumbros.

## Members

### In the manuscript

- [[Gweine Ptorhan]]: Hylvisi; regent

## Married into the Line

- [[Ztiodyn Ihryl]] (partner of [[Gweine Ptorhan]])
