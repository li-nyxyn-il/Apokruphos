# Brenthausen Audsen I

> [!NOTE] Name check
> The manuscript spells him **Brenthausen Audsen I**. Rename this note in Obsidian to match.

## Description

The [[Grymsol Elohaim]] of the Umbral Throne and Aiden's grandfather, father of [[Havil Audsen]]. When his heir [[Brent Audsen II|Brenthausen Audsen II]] dies suddenly, he names his great-great-grandson [[Danyal Audsen]] heir instead. That decree sends [[Kanyn Audsen]] and [[Donald Audsen]] into flight and raises fears of a succession war. He co-signs Aiden's dictate of marriage.
