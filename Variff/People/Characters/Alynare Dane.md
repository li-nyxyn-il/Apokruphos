# Alynare Dane

## Description

Head of [[Line of Dane]], wife of [[Havil Audsen]] and mother of [[Halion Dane]], [[Elyssa Dane]], [[Erynalda Dane]], [[Sunie Dane]], [[Aiden Dane]] and [[Yniel Dane]]. She is tall and slender and always veiled in dark lace. Behind the veil are maroon "blood eyes" like red circles on onyx, whose pupils lighten where others' darken. She speaks with an accent no one else has, deals in riddles "as opaque as her veil", and spent less time raising each child than the one before.

Hesseh tells Aiden her history. She was born in Shentz at Heedhaim and orphaned at six when a fire leveled the city. The Pluker Grymsol sent her to the Morghanes at Aynhaim to be raised in the ways of the [[Khaska|Khaskylil]]. Ten years later that city fell to Koloudian slaves, and she fled north to Hale to serve the Audsen court. The Price khronologies, though, name a "Sigres Dane" as wife of [[Wyranoz Price]] in the 400s, great-great-grandmother of Emanda. When the bear Ursulus confronts Sunie, it recites "Ayalon, Ythinyx, Sunie, Sigres, Helyn, Alynare", tying her mother's names together.

In 557 she arranges Aiden's sacrificial union, convenes a secret council with [[Xerand Leyd]], a Koloudian emissary and [[Ihlven Ihryl]], and enlists Sunie as her instrument.
