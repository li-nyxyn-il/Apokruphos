# Gweine Ptorhan

## Description

Daughter of Hylvaer Fuhlen Ptorhan, governor of Ptian, and the recently widowed wife of [[Ztiodyn Ihryl]]. She serves as regent in Vinhrest for their infant son [[Zhacheri Ihryl]].
