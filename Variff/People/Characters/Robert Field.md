# Robert Field

## Description

Grymsol Pynumbros of [[Maphlia]], seated at Klate, whom Havil scorns as "the Boy of Maphlia" and a "pitiable, red king". His father, [[Robert Field the Old]], kept him hidden from public eyes. Now grown, he is sickly, often recovering from illness and "too sick to walk the Path". Following Aphyk tradition, he married his own sister, [[Julene Plukur]]. [[Ghlenn Jhoryll]] is widely thought to rule through him. His "gift" to Havil at Hyl'viati is a gilded box of vipers holding [[Ohsymondi Burch]]'s severed head.
