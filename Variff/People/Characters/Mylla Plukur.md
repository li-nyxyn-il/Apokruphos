# Mylla Plukur

## Description

The Nivisi Mylla, sister of [[Anne Plukur]] and [[Robert Field]]: a girl perhaps younger than Yniel, with pale skin, beady pale-blue eyes and small yellowing teeth. [[Ghlenn Jhoryll]] offers her to Aiden as a wife if he will abandon the Pylgrymage.
