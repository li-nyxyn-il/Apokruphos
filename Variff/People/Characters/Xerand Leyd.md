# Xerand Leyd

## Description

A Koloudian raised in the desert who married into a Maphyk Line, Hylvaer of the Leyd and Lord of Mren. His wife is [[Sali Morwen]], which makes him brother-by-law to the Governess [[Elyssa Morwen]] of the Vyle. His accent betrays his upbringing, though he can hide it under a Maphlian tenor. Sunie calls him "that Koloudian fox": he reads her Khaskylil game and plays it better.

At the departure feast he gives Aiden the white-gold saber Niaviopti, "flaming tooth", and tells him to ask its history at the Krypt of Drake. He sits on [[Alynare Dane]]'s secret council, and Sunie later reports that he tried to turn the Koloudian conflict into a marriage alliance with Ayn'daiun. He leads Sunie's pursuit of [[Yniel Dane]]'s abductor, only for her to find him at the fire beside [[Ihlven Ihryl]], her sister bound and gagged. He insists he is "still a friend of Dane" and that they are only taking Yniel "off of the game board" before Alynare can marry her off for "a mummer's peace".
