# Havil Audsen

## Description

Hylvaer Governor of [[Phorva]], ruling from [[Maphlamar]], and son of the Grymsol Elohaim [[Brentausen Audsen I|Brenthausen Audsen I]]. He is harsh-looking: gaunt in the cheeks, wrinkled at the brow, pale and growing paler each year in the south. His eyes have faded from crystal blue to "the color of ice". He keeps his hair short and his face shaved, and his nose is "so narrow it might cut rope". What frightened Bleyk as a boy was not his anger but his "not-quite-angry, brooding simmer".

He married [[Alynare Dane]] out of what he calls "my duty and my sacrifice" and, in Bleyk's eyes, she has absorbed his reputation, his honor and his will. He privately despises the boy-king [[Robert Field]] and his counselor [[Ghlenn Jhoryll]]. At the end of Part Two he is camped before Hyl'viati when [[Thanyel Field]] arrives with the Grymsol's "gift".
