# Robert Field the Old

## Description

Called "Robert the Old" in the manuscript. He ruled Maphlia until his death three years before the story and took the Aphyk marriage rites with [[Lyllyth Plukur]]. He kept his son [[Robert Field]] hidden from public eyes.
