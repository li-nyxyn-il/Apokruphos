# Kassia Price

> [!NOTE] Name check
> The family tree spells her **Cassia**. The manuscript uses **Kassia**.

## Description

Daughter of [[Eyhdroz Price]] and sister of [[Williem Price]], recorded in the Price khronologies. In the family tree she is the late wife of [[Daldos Belravi|Daldoz Belravi]] and mother of [[Sofia Belravi]], [[Brienwyn Belravi]] and [[Kheinyn Belravi]], which makes the Belravi siblings Alynare's great-grandchildren.
