# Railus Dane

## Description

Son of [[Halion Dane]] and [[Eylla Burch]]. Like his sister [[Iyaeli Dane]] he favors his mother, with brown eyes and straight black hair. Sunie takes the two of them to play with Bleyk's children in the estate gardens.
