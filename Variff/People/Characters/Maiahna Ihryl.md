# Maiahna Ihryl

## Description

Wife of [[Jhyn Price]], sister of [[Ihlven Ihryl]] and [[Ztiodyn Ihryl]], and mother of [[Jenifyr Price]] and [[Demien Price]]. She was born 512 and died by immolation in 557, alongside her son. Demien had her silver hair; in Jenifyr's dream both of them float in the deep in robes of dark silk. Ihlven "stokes the flame of Maiahna's memory every day".
