# Halion Dane

## Description

Eldest son of [[Alynare Dane]] and [[Havil Audsen]], prefect of [[Seawalt 1|the Seawalt]], with curly hair and the family's green eyes. His mother "married up", and the union she arranged for him with [[Eylla Burch]] is "a conjugation of duty", forced on him by his father. He does not hate Eylla, but does not much love her either. He leaves her and their children [[Railus Dane]] and [[Iyaeli Dane]] behind for the Pynumbral Summit in Penhyla, where he drinks in the Seriast baths. There he meets the yellow-eyed blue-blood stranger who asks which god he worships. He ignores Sunie. At the summit's masquerade he dances with [[Jenifyr Price]]. Narrator Aiden later calls him "now dead and damned".
