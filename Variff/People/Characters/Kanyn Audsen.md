## Description

Son of [[Brent Audsen II|Brenthausen Audsen II]] and [[Cassa Audsen|Kassa Audsen]]. When [[Danyal Audsen]] is named heir, Kanyn flees across the Ebyn Bay to Porthelm, and Sunie's sister fears he will challenge the succession.
