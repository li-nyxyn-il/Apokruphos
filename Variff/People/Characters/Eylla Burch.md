# Eylla Burch

> [!NOTE] Name check
> Your 2024 notes spelled her **Ellya**. The manuscript uses **Eylla**.

## Description

Daughter of Prefect [[Oluydius Burch]] of Korvus and [[Myhnaela Sale]], and wife of [[Halion Dane]] at the Seawalt, in a union "of practical appeasement" arranged by Alynare and forced by Havil. She is mother of [[Railus Dane]] and [[Iyaeli Dane]]. Her father is fond of her and eager to visit his grandchildren.
