# Erynalda Dane

## Description

Second daughter of [[Alynare Dane]] and [[Havil Audsen]], called Eryn. She lives in Penhyla with her husband and hosted Halion during the Pynumbral Summit. Sunie thinks "she looks more Audsen than Dane". As a girl she was the one who carried a frightened [[Yniel Dane]] to bed. A letter from Eryn may lie behind Alynare's "certain realities" in Part Two.
