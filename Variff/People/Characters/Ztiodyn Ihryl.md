# Ztiodyn Ihryl

## Description

The late Grymsol Pynumbros of Ptormia, brother of [[Ihlven Ihryl]] and [[Maiahna Ihryl]]. His death passed the throne to his infant son [[Zhacheri Ihryl]], with his widow [[Gweine Ptorhan]] as regent, and left the Ihryl Line "shattered".
