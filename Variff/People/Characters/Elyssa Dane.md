# Elyssa Dane

## Description

Oldest daughter of [[Alynare Dane]] and [[Havil Audsen]], married at Klate to [[Thanyel Field]], cousin of the Grymsol Pynumbros of Maphlia. She was always closer to her mother than the younger children were. She embraces Aiden when he arrives at Klate and tells him of the Price disgrace, but she knew of [[Ghlenn Jhoryll]]'s plan to marry him to Mylla and did not warn him. "You have family here, brother. Out there is only death." By Part Two her position in Klate is precarious.
