# Aiden Dane

## Description

The narrator of *A Pylgrym's Plight* and the youngest son of [[Havil Audsen]] and [[Alynare Dane]]. He has the green eyes all of Alynare's children share, and he is happiest hunched over parchment in his room. His siblings call him arrogant and too clever for his own good. He was never given the Audsen name, which he feels keenly: his father gave him only the honor of being a Dane.

His mother arranges a sacrificial union between Aiden and [[Emanda Price]] of Ptorm, which sends him down the Path of the Pylgrymage with Commander Phylip, the cartographer Samule Dyk, the hunter [[Jeyk Audsen|Jeyk]], the Hylvannus Hesseh and his Legionnaire Lyanir. At Klate, [[Ghlenn Jhoryll]] tries to talk him out of the marriage and into wedding [[Mylla Plukur]] instead; Aiden refuses and journeys on. At [[Hylvynhani Falls]] he meets Emanda, with [[Sofia Belravi]] as her translator. He falls for Sofia and begins learning ptormyk from her.

In the khronologies he uncovers two secrets. The Ptormyk Lines appear to sacrifice their firstborn heirs in infancy, which reframes what a "Sacrificial Union" asks of him. And Emanda's great-great-grandmother was a Dane, "Sigres Dane". [[Xerand Leyd]]'s gift, the Shentz blade Niaviopti ("flaming tooth"), and the riddle of [[Mehania]] follow him toward the Krypt of [[Drake]].
