# Oluydius Burch

## Description

Hylvaer Prefect of Korvus, head of a minor Line under the governorship of [[Phorva]] (and so under Havil). He is father of [[Eylla Burch]] and [[Ohsymondi Burch]] and husband of [[Myhnaela Sale]]. He welcomes Aiden's company warmly on the road south and later presses Havil to let Ohsymondi court a Dane daughter, which Alynare reads as Burch overstepping "in his play to integrate his Line into mine". His younger brother Reman Burch sits on Alynare's council.
