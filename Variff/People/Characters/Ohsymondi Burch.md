# Ohsymondi Burch

## Description

Son of Prefect [[Oluydius Burch]] of Korvus and [[Myhnaela Sale]], and brother of [[Eylla Burch]]. He is slightly younger than Sunie and "far more handsome than his father", and is kind enough to carry Aiden's last message to Yniel. He shares a young flame with [[Yniel Dane]]. Oluydius hopes to marry him into the Dane family, and Alynare forbids it. His severed head arrives among three vipers in the gilded box [[Thanyel Field]] delivers to Havil's tent as the Grymsol's "gift": "just another of the dead".
