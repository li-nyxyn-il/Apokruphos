# Ghlenn Jhoryll

## Description

Hylvaer Governor and "right hand of the Pynumbros" of Maphlia at Klate: a skeleton of a man with thinning gray hair and a deeply wrinkled face. He is a cousin of the late Grymsol [[Eddyson Field]] and husband of [[Anne Plukur]]. Over breakfast he humiliates Aiden about the Price disgrace, then proposes that he abandon the Pylgrymage and marry [[Mylla Plukur]] under Aphyk Primogeniture. Havil calls him the "foul odor of a man who whispers rot" into the boy-king's ear.
