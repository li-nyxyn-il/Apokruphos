# Danyal Audsen

## Description

The Nivaer Danyal of the Line Audsen, son of [[Klade Aulden]] and [[Meri Audsen]]. On the death of [[Brent Audsen II|Brenthausen Audsen II]] he is named heir to the Umbral Throne, though Sunie has never heard of him.
