# Brent Audsen

## Description

Bleyk's son, "Little Brent", six years old and formally the Nivaer Brenthousen Audsen. He has wispy golden curls, "the curls of the father", bright blue eyes and a toothless grin. He clings to Bleyk's leg at every parting and once shoves his sister [[Belise Audsen]] into the grass hard enough to bloody her brow.
