# Jeyk of the Varivs

> [!NOTE] Name check
> Your 2024 notes filed him under House Audsen. The manuscript calls him **Jeyk of the Varivs**, a lowborn hunter, so he isn't listed in [[Line of Audsen]]. Consider renaming this note in Obsidian.

## Description

Introduced at the feast of departure as "Vaer Jeyk, of… the Varivs. Hunter from the north", a lowborn foreigner with "the thickest Shentzlian accent" Sunie has ever heard. He addresses his offering to [[Alynare Dane]]: a polished black stone the size of a fist, flecked with cloudy white. He asks only to journey with Aiden's party "that I may atone for my slight against his honor", and she grants it. He then vanishes from the hall "as if he had never even lived". He is a man of middling years, "hardly a day over thirty", with a short beard on a gaunt jaw and slick brown hair. He rides silently with Aiden's company, translates ptormyk for [[Emanda Price]], and gives Aiden the maxim "A wise man need not change his path, for he chooses it knowing where it will lead."

## From your planning notes

Jeyk is the character readers most connect to the "old ways". Part Three has interludes titled "Jeyk of the Varivs" and "Jeyk the Drake".
