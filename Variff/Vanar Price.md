# Vanar Price

## Description

Younger son of [[Williem Price]], born 517, Hylvaer and Lord of Erudztorg of the Atzh'ptorm. He becomes his father's heir once [[Jhyn Price]] is disgraced. He looks much like Jhyn, with the same long, narrow face, sunken cheeks and sharp nose, plus a short auburn beard and clay-colored braided hair shaved at the sides. His eyes are a paler red, "like skin raw from a burn", and he wears a purple cloak.

He hires Ythaniel, "the Killer", on the Ecumenci's behalf and rides with Zorn's host and the Mountain Lion. On the ship at Ptianhyliv he corners his brother and niece, offers Jhyn his freedom in exchange for [[Jenifyr Price]], and beats her until Jhyn returns. He is married to [[Bieyhana Reid]]; their son [[Williem Price II]] died the year he was born, and their daughter is [[Emanda Price]].
