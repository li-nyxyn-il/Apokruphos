# Williem Price

## Description

Patron of the Price Line and Governor of the Atzh'ptorm of Ptormia (the province of Vhitzni), born 482. He is first servant to the infant Grymsol Pynumbros [[Zhacheri Ihryl]] and holds "powerful sway in Vinhrest". His wife [[Zayna Krete]] died of illness in 553. He is father of [[Jhyn Price]] and [[Vanar Price]], and grandson of Sigres Dane, who is [[Alynare Dane]]. He co-signs Aiden's dictate of marriage, and it is he who sends Vanar to hire Ythaniel.
