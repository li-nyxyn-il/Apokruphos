Atzch (yhlgori): Valley 

hylvynhani: Hyl- high place, peak, mountain top; vyn- river; hani- descent, lowering, made low. High river made low, (Ylhgori) 

meha nia (Shentzlian): Cold Flame

helm (vinhili): land

haim (shentzlian): port

vin (vinhili): mountain

grotz, ztorg (vinhili): beginning / end often used to denote the headwaters or mouth of a river or stream, also used as a modifier on the names of cities to denote an inland or coastal port

mehania (yhlgori): meh- the heavens. literally the heavens brought low.

varigon (ecumene)- the process of an abstract idea being made concrete; from Varif- idea, gon- stone

phionyx (shentzlian)- god of life; phio- god, nyx- life

ythranyx, ythinyx, masculine/feminine (shentzlian)- who was dead come alive

draci, dici- m/f (shentzlian) - royalty, with implication that such royalty is divine in nature
