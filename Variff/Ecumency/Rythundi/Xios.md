# Xios

One of the Rythundi Maph, the dead gods of [[Maphlia]] venerated by custom beneath the Nymuni [[Khaska]].

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** [[Maphlia]]
- **Nymuni:** [[Khaska]]
- **Status:** rythundi, a god *nogirav*. "Their dominia, designatia, and dictates are forfeit in their death" (Sunie).

## Dictate

- Maphyk soldiers carry pocket idols of him into battle.

## Designature

- Law. "Xios was the god of law."

- Appears in: "Sunie: Khaskylil" · "Sunie: The Mother’s Voice"
