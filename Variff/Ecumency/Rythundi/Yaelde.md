# Yaelde

One of the Rythundi Maph, the dead gods of [[Maphlia]] venerated by custom beneath the Nymuni [[Khaska]].

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** [[Maphlia]]
- **Nymuni:** [[Khaska]]
- **Status:** rythundi, a god *nogirav*. "Their dominia, designatia, and dictates are forfeit in their death" (Sunie).

## Dictate

- Venerated by custom; no rite is described yet.

## Designature

- Time, or history. Sunie can't remember which, and Rhynise answers: "History, time, there is no difference."

- Appears in: "Sunie: Khaskylil"
