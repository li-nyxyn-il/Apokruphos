# Jhunus

The creator, and a strange case among the [[Rythundi]]: a dead god tied to no Pynumbra. The Ecumenci assembled him from various creation myths and set him into their Monomyth of the Muni.

## Names

- **Jhunus:** his ecumenycal name.
- **Jhun:** in Aphyk and Maphyk lands. Sunie thinks of "all of Jhun's creation".
- **Jhunz:** in Shentz and Ptormyk lands, where the word can also mean creation itself. The glossary in Part Two has "Jhunz - creation (shentz)". The snake inscription at Atzhaim reads *ul phionyx ie ul spriax ztiel varif itziel krotz ztie ul jhunz*: "…which makes time, the maker of creation."
- The spelling differences are still being ironed out.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** none. He is a rythundi of the whole Ecumenci rather than of one land.
- **Texts:** the Ecumenci's Monomyth of the Muni, and the ptormyk Solyloqui of the Mantle.
- **Status:** dead. In the Solyloqui he made the world "before his death the last".

## Dictate

- The Solyloqui of the Mantle (as Demien quotes it): "So Jhunus breathed a mote of dirt and a mote of light into the marble of the world before his death the last, and ripped from the seas their land." He tethered the rising fires with "a cord woven from the fibres of life". He doused the fiery pits with water to make the sea. From his work came the soiule of light and the soiule of soiule, [[Jhaspyr]] and [[Ilys]].
- "Jhunus molded the nymuni, and he sent them to the sky… From the stars they fell rythundi, and the mountains varigon their tombs."
- The Askiamaz say the leaguestones were carved from the summit of Utzkva'hjun "by the spraits of Jhunz" (Jeyk).

## Designature

- Creation itself, the shaping of the world and of the gods.
- The mountains: the peaks above [[Hylvynhani Falls]] are "the works of Jhunus", the Creator's Crown.
- The stars: the constellation Jhunus, which smoke swallows in "Ythaniel: Sacrifice", "robbing the creator of all his works". Also Creation's Chisel, a twinkling red dagger, which the Blood Sage wields.
