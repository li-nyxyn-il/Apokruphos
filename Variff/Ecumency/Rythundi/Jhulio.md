# Jhulio

A dead god (rythundi) whose idol Maphyk soldiers carry into battle alongside Xios and Khunxar, so most likely one of the Rythundi of [[Maphlia]], beneath the Nymuni [[Khaska]]. Sunie doesn't name Jhulio in her list of the Rythundi Maph.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** [[Maphlia]] (inferred from who venerates it)
- **Nymuni:** [[Khaska]]
- **Status:** rythundi, a god *nogirav*. "Their dominia, designatia, and dictates are forfeit in their death" (Sunie).

## Dictate

- Maphyk soldiers carry pocket idols of Jhulio into battle.

- Not stated.
