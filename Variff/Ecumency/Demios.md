# Demios

> [!NOTE]
> Demios was in earlier versions of the manuscript. In the current draft he appears only by his title, "the Lord of Fear and Frenzy", in the Prologue.

Lord of Fear and Frenzy. The manuscript describes him without naming him.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** your 2024 notes make him Chrysthitian.
- **City temple:** not yet named.
- **Atzhaim:** his is one of the two Krypts missing from Atzhaim's seven, along with [[Baldyro]]'s (see [[Ecumency of the Ten Beyonds]]).

## Dictate

- "The Lord of Fear and Frenzy substitutes terror for ritual and robs men of that great sacrament, for no emotion can be so great as fear" (the stranger in the Penhyla baths).

## Designature

- Fear and frenzy. Your 2024 notes add storms.

## Earlier notes (2024)

Your 2024 notes: lord of frenzy, Chrysthitian god of storms, god of fear.
