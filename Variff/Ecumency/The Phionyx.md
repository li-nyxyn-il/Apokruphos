# The Phionyx

One of the two Kairal Gods of old Shentz: Beyond of life, the valleys and beginnings. The giver of the *meha nia*, the Cold Flame, and the god of the [[Nyxians]].

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** Shentz ([[Shentzlia]]). He was revered above all other old Shentzlian gods, together with [[Drake]]. He is also the god of the Nyxian blue-bloods of [[Bluestone]], who worship "in the old ways". To the [[Kourzechi]] he is [[Xyioxios]] (see [[Ancient Lore]]).
- **City temple:** Atzhaim. The Krypt of Phionyx stands as a lone cathedral on a butte at the city's heart beside the lake docks, with "the largest nedia floor in all the Umbara", a vast library, schools and a hospital: "the truest ideal of the City-Krypt". [[Atzch Lake Hall]], on [[Lake Shentzlia]], was Atzhaim's original name and spelling (your 2024 [[Temple of Phionyx]] note).

## Dictate

- The Seven Mysteries (see [[The Cold Flame of The Phionyx]]). For example, the mystery of the genisys: "For mortals to bring new life into this world is sin… yet still, we are commanded to this sacred duty." Also: "there is no mortal act which is not sin".
- The *meha nia* is his gift: "Phionyx alone may raise the dead to life again." The Belravi say it is what the blind man must claim.
- The stars are "the instrument through which Phionyx speaks to his people", to be read, not worshipped. "Phionyx does not speak from the summit."
- "Phionyx always has his price." Old-ways worshippers are "spared little love by the other peoples of this land".
- Marriage between Pynumbra ends here in the Rites of Consummation. The bride stays cloistered in the Krypt until she is with child.

## Designature

- Life, the valleys, beginnings, fertility and bounty, and the future on the stream of time (Hesseh). Your 2024 notes: creation, life, fertility and seasons.
- Etymology: *phio*, god; *nyx*, life. The red falcon is his image.

## From your planning notes

Phionyx is one of the four superclusters. Nyxianism has split into "old ways" Nyxians (Daldoz) and Mehanians (the Mountain Lion).

## Earlier notes (2024)

Your 2024 notes: the ancient Shentzi god of creation, life, fertility and seasons. His Prelate is the only one who does not also head a lesser pantheon. [[Ancient Lore]] makes the Phionyx the source of Shentzlian vampirism, the Cold Flame.
