# Mehania

The tenth Beyond in your 2024 notes, the one without a temple. In the manuscript, *mehania* is above all a word and a riddle: the high made low, the reciprocant of *ainahem*.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** Yhlgori (2024 notes).
- **City temple:** none. Your 2024 notes make her the only Beyond without a Prelate or temple on the Pylgrymage. This fits Hesseh's Atzhaim: "nine krypts for nine Beyonds" on the "Great Path of the Ten Beyonds".

## Dictate

- Vakirros's words, kept as sacred at the Krypt of Grymsiere: "Thus do I say, I shall be reborn in Mehania."
- "Mehania is intrinsically linked to" the Kairal Dichotomy, "studied in the nedia of Atzhaim" (Hesseh).

## Designature

- "Mehania: high varigon low." It is the mountain, the tomb and the grave, against *ainahem*, the valley and the womb.
- "Mehania of station and mehania of temporum may be distinct… the Yhlmahni are certainly the mehania of the Yhlgori" (Perythtyrep).
- Your [[Dictionary]]: *meh-*, the heavens; "literally the heavens brought low".

## From your planning notes

"Mehania" is ambiguous by design, with several parallel meanings, and can refer to more than one person (see [[Mehania Dane]], [[Mehania Belravi]]). Mehania is one of the four superclusters, and Mehanians are the Nyxians opposed to the "old ways".

## Earlier notes (2024)

Your 2024 notes: the Yhlgori goddess of darkness and night. In [[Ancient Lore]], the Yhlgori believed Mehania a place, then a person; see [[Mehania Dane]].
