# The Grymsiere

Beyond of the blood of the Lines and the soil of their graves. He is the divine ancestor of the Yhlgori, whose "voice" the conqueror Vakirros became.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** Yhlgori in origin. "Grymsiere reigned in the Yhlgori pantheon long before their conquests and reigned still the same, as a Beyond, long after the Conqueror's death." Through the conquest he became the uniting god of the whole Umbara.
- **City temple:** [[Yhlgrym]]: the Krypt of Grymsiere and its small collection of towers and courtyards on the nedia hyl, among scattered farms. Its Hylvannus Kraiark is Perythtyrep, and a Kraiark Prelate of History speaks there. Your 2024 [[Temple of Grymsiere]] note sets it at the south end of the [[Great Atzch Road]].
- **Other krypts:** the outermost district of Atzhaim serves his Krypt.

## Dictate

- His Krypt keeps the Lines: "we of his Krypt are the keepers of these Lines". It holds the Ecumenycal Khronologies where Aiden reads the records of every Line.
- The marriage Pylgrymage between Pynumbra begins at his city temple, "united by the sword of the Grymsiere, by his blood, and by his soil".
- His blessing: "May he bless the blood of your veins and the soil of your feat… that you may walk in pain."
- Grymsol monologi teaches that Vakirros was not divine but "merely imbued with this divine spirit". "Vakirros Grymsiere became Vakirros Grymsol, not he the former." Vakirros's sacred words are kept there: "Thus do I say, I shall be reborn in Mehania."

## Designature

- "Grymsiere's domain is the blood of the Lines and the soil of their graves." Your 2024 notes: blood and earth.
- The Blood Sage constellation, "that icon of Grymsiere", which rose the night Vakirros marched north.

## As others see it

- Perythtyrep speaks of "the fall of the Grymsiere Line" as one of the many tragedies of the land.

## From your planning notes

Grymsiere is one of the four superclusters, alongside Phionyx, Drake/Ayalon and Mehania.
