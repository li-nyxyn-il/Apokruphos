# Khaska

The Raven Mother, Beyond of wisdom and Nymuni of Maphlia. Her women, the Khaskylil, train in the sophystri that is her worship.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** [[Maphlia]]. Your 2024 notes call her the [[Maphian|Maphlian]] goddess. She is the living god above the Rythundi Maph (see below).
- **City temple:** Khoratzh ([[Choratzch]] in your 2024 notes). Hesseh trained at its nedia, where "the temple of the Raven Mother stands tall atop the summit of the Hyl'nuizt Eltzi", overlooking the Great Valley of Shentz from its northernmost finger. He calls it "the Raven's nest".
- **Other krypts:** the Krypt of Khaska in [[Maphlamar]]'s inner district: towers, a library, a cathedral and the Khaskylil's tower. The Khaska Krypt and Library of Klate together enclose that city's nedia. A Krypt to Khaska stands in Atzhaim's second district.
- **Worshippers:** the Audsen-Dane household ("the Raven and the Crow", the gods of Havil's family and Line) and the Maphyk host.

## Dictate

- Her worship is the *practice* of wisdom, not the keeping of knowledge. "Khaska's mote is her wisdom, and in worship of her, we make this mote varigon" (the Lady Rhynise).
- The Khaskylil, her order of women sophysts, train in statecraft, "polyssian observance and the tapping of secrets", and the molding of memory, "the truest form of her worship". Members named so far: the Lady Rhynise, Sister Unduna, Sister Ynsisys.
- Her Kraiarks wear sand wool trimmed with gold and embroidered with ravens and trees (Hesseh), and teach language, law, history and monologi.
- Before a Maphyk battle her Kraiark and a khaskyli join the Kraiark of [[Khodos]] in a joint rite. "Your Mother and your Father shall be wed in this flame". Commanders anoint a goat ("With the Mother's blood I bathe this offering"), it is immolated, and the khaskyli declares "The Mother's voice rides with you today, my sons."
- "To worship the Raven Mother is to worship all the Beyonds."

## Designature

- Wisdom and sophystri ("is not sophystri the dominion of Khaska?"). Your 2024 notes add teaching.
- She still "held council to those sons the Father claimed", but on the field of war "she had less power than even the Rythundi".
- The Raven constellation ("the Raven fell from her perch").

## Rythundi of Maphlia

The Polimunus Rythundi venerated beneath Khaska in Maphlia (see [[Rythundi]]):

- [[Khunxar]]: The soil
- [[Denma]]: The seasons
- [[Ithkria]]: The rivers and the seas
- [[Xios]]: Law
- [[Yaelde]]: Time, or history
- [[Sysil]]: Courage, judging by the soldiers' prayer (likely Maphlian)
- [[Jhulio]]: Not stated (likely Maphlian)
- [[Earaen]]: Not stated (likely Maphlian)

## As others see it

- To the stranger in the Penhyla baths, "the Mother of Wisdom makes all men fools by elevating the women of the Khaskylil". Halion calls her worshippers "women who whisper falsehoods and call it wisdom".
- "Ask a Kaskaedian and he will tell you Khaska is a witch-queen worshiped by devils" (Perythtyrep).
- Common oath: "by Khaska's claws".

## Earlier notes (2024)

Your 2024 [[Ecumency of the Ten Beyonds]] note calls her the Raven Mother, Maphlian goddess of wisdom and teaching. [[Ancient Lore]] traces the Raven Mother and Crow Father to the Kourzechi myth of [[Xyioxios]], and has them grant blue-blooded nobles wisdom or might.
