# Khodos

The Crow Father, Beyond of war. He is wed to [[Khaska]] in the war rites of the Maphyk host.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** your 2024 notes make him the [[Aphian]] god. In the manuscript he and Khaska are "gods of my family, gods of my Line" to the Audsen-Dane children, and Havil prays "to the Raven and to the Crow".
- **City temple:** [[Aldyngrad]] in the far north. Your note is spelled Aldyngrad; you've also written Alyngrad.
- **Other krypts:** a Krypt to Khodos stands in Atzhaim's second district alongside Khaska's.

## Dictate

- "The Father of War subdues his servants by instituting dictates by which alone men may battle", making war a matter of "law and magistracy" (the stranger in the Penhyla baths).
- In the pre-battle rite his Kraiark sprinkles dust that makes the fire flare, lights the torch for Khaska's Kraiark, and cuts the participants' palms. The commanders swear "With the Father's blood I bathe this offering."
- The war dead are his offering and crows "his ministers". "When blood was spilled by sword and spear, all who took part worshiped the God of War."

## Designature

- War. Your 2024 notes add famine and sickness.
- The Crow constellation, which dances beside the Raven.

## As others see it

- Halion asks "who are men who have never seen war to make worship of Khodos?"
- Bleyk in the war camp is "a son of Khodos".
