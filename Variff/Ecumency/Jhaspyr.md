# Jhaspyr

The Son of Sun, Beyond of the day and twin of [[Ilys]]. His Law governs marriage without the sacrifice of heirs.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** Ptormia (the Atzh'ptorm) in the manuscript. Your 2024 notes made Jhaspyr a [[Vhinhili]] goddess. Jhaspyr and Ilys "both rested" on the twin peaks of the Atzh'ptivisos in Ptorm "before the conquerors raised our dead to such-called life".
- **City temple:** [[Ptormvos]], whose nedia to Jhaspyr is Kraiark Norvron's. Norvron is also called "a Kraiark of the Krypt of Jhaspyr in Erudztorg": the draft conflates Ptormvos and Erudztorg, which are still to be reconciled.
- **Other krypts:** a Krypt to Jhaspyr in Ptianhyliv (its acolytes wear gray) and one in Atzhaim.

## Dictate

- The Law of Jhaspyr is the marriage law under which Aiden and Emanda are wed, "for the primogeniture of the eldest male as heir". "Jhaspyr Law, Nivaer, not Ilyk. No babes will be butchered in their cradle."
- Dawn prayer, from "many traditions of the Son's worship": *biyoule rykiyhot um stvoh*, "Jhaspyr's light strikes the soul aflame".
- Norvron's acolytes wear "robes of Jhaspyr orange". His Kraiarks, with Ilys's, once tried to translate the ptormyk Solyloqui into ecumene.

## Designature

- The sun and the day: "the soiule of light held dominion over day" in the creation of [[Jhunus]] (the Solyloqui of the Mantle).
- The light of the rising sun, which kindles the soul. "The Son of Sun may rule the day, sister, but the Daughter of Moon rules the night."

## Earlier notes (2024)

Your 2024 notes: the Vhinhili goddess of the sun, nature and change, twin to Ilys. In earlier versions Jhaspyr and Ilys were twin sister gods. The current draft makes Jhaspyr male, the Son of Sun.
