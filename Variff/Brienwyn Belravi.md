# Brienwyn Belravi

## Description

A Belravi woman, daughter of [[Daldos Belravi|Daldoz Belravi]] and [[Kassia Price]], who tends the blind man at [[Bluestone]] (see [[Demien Price]]). She calls him "love" and gives him the drink that carries him out of the world. She keeps a bird that answers to her clicks and wears a heavy perfume. Her skin is "smooth as silk and softer than snow", and in his visions her eyes are purple. When he collapses in the snow, she wraps him in her cloak.
