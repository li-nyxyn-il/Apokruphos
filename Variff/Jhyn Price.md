# Jhyn Price

> [!NOTE] Name check
> The family tree spells him **Jhin Price**. The manuscript and your earlier notes use **Jhyn**, so this note keeps Jhyn.

## Description

Firstborn son of [[Williem Price]] and a Hylvaer of Ptorm, born 507. He is known "for its utmost obstinacy" in refusing every suitor for his daughter [[Jenifyr Price]]. He has blood eyes, a violent widow's peak and a wild, braided orange mane, and his cheeks carry scars from his flight. He worships [[The Phionyx]] rather than any god of Ptorm, and rumors of that "blasphemous" Nyxian faith ruin him: Elyssa reports that he was accused of incest with his daughter and fled with her into hiding.

His wife [[Maiahna Ihryl]] and son [[Demien Price]] died by immolation in 557. The khronologies record Jhyn himself as dead "of unknown causes". He flees with the pregnant Jenifyr through Ptianhyliv, fights off his brother [[Vanar Price]] at the docks, and searches for a hidden valley "by Phionyx's mercy". Grief hollowed him, then "built a furnace" of rage and zeal.
