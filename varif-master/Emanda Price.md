# Emanda Price

## Description

Daughter of [[Vanar Price]] and [[Bieyhana Reid]], born 542 z.d.y., and Aiden's betrothed in the sacrificial union. A "matchstick of red hair" and red eyes; she speaks only ptormyk, so [[Sofia Belravi]] or [[Jeyk Audsen|Jeyk]] must translate. Everything she does seems a performance at first, a "smile that seemed to hide behind itself", and Aiden repeatedly offends her without knowing how. When he finally answers her in her own tongue, she gives "the brightest smile" he has ever seen.

Her older brother [[Williem Price II]] died the year he was born. Aiden comes to suspect that firstborn death was no accident.
