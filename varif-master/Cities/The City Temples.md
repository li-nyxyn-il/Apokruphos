**Each of the cities along the [[Great Atzch Road]] are city-states under the jurisdiction of the Ecumency. A governor appointed by the temple prelate rules the civil affairs while the prelate himself governs over the religious affairs. Each city has three main establishments, the city court, the city temple, and related to the temple yet independent in its own right, the scholastic [[nedia]], an institution of learning with a special focus tied to the head of the sub-pantheon for which the city is dedicated. In this way, scholarly pursuits are to the Ecumency an extension of Ecumenical affairs, intimately tied together with religion. 

  

[[Yhlgrym]] and the [[Temple of Grymsiere]] are located at the southern end of the Great Atzch Road along the Yhlgori river in [[Yhl Water Gap]], the southernmost region of Atzchia, not far from [[Hylvynhani Falls]]. The Nedic focus of Yhlgrym is History, and [[Ecumenical Monology]]. 

[[Atzch Lake Hall]] and the [[Temple of Phionyx]] are located on the southern tip of [[Lake Shentzlia]], the repository of the Shentlian watershed and source of the Yhlgori river. The [[Nedic Schools]] of Atzch Lake Hall are fertility and genealogy, and climatology.
