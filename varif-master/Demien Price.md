# Demien Price

## Description

Twin brother of [[Jenifyr Price]] and son of [[Jhyn Price]] and [[Maiahna Ihryl]]. He has his mother's silver hair and a poet's inward gaze. Jenifyr recites his verses, and remembers his saying that "there is no better liar than the memory of a dream". The Price khronologies list two Demiens: one born and dead in 525 "of natural causes", and the twin born 531 and dead by immolation in 557 alongside his mother.

After that death he wakes at [[Bluestone]] as the blind, nameless "dead man", Ythranyx. He is tended by [[Brienwyn Belravi]], overseen by [[Daldos Belravi|Daldoz Belravi]] and trained by [[Kheinyn Belravi]].
