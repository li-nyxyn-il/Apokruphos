Reading notes

  • Shared symbols. f and ph, i and y, and k and c each use one symbol.
  • Writing box. It reads hl, ie, ph, pt, qu, sh and th as one symbol each, writes c as k, and lists any character that has no symbol.
  • Two i sounds. The i / y symbol is the sound in sin, spelled with a y. The ie symbol is the sound in sight and tried. The box writes every y with the i / y symbol and every i or ie with the ie symbol.
  • Sound pairs. hl, ie, pt, qu, sh and th have symbols of their own.
