# Ilys

The Daughter of Moon, Beyond of night, the stars and dreams, and twin of [[Jhaspyr]]. Her Law demands the firstborn.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** Ptormia, and the clans of the Vin ([[Vhinhelm]]), where "the Il'hyo are the holy men". Your 2024 notes make her [[Ptormic]].
- **City temple:** [[Yirhelm]], "that city-a-temple unto the god Ilys", where Jenifyr and Demien grew up. There is a krypt of Ilys in the Atzh'ptorm, whose emissary meets Zorn's pack, and a Krypt to Ilys in Atzhaim.

## Dictate

- The Law of Ilys (Ilyk Law) is the Ptormyk marriage custom under which firstborn heirs die in infancy, "a documented and longstanding religious practice under the laws of Ilys". "Ask a Ptormyk and he will tell you the children are spared the cruelty of the world and given a seat with Ilys in paradise."
- Among the Vinhili clans, Zorn's pack burns children and eats them in her name at the full moon, singing "songs of rapture to Ilys's beauty".
- "The sun has set, daughter of Ilys, and so the dictate passes."

## Designature

- The moon and the night: "the soiule of soiule dominion over night" in the creation of [[Jhunus]].
- The celestia of stars, "the lesser, wider, darker domain of Ilys".
- Dreams: "Ilys gives you dreams" (Jenifyr, whose god she is).

## As others see it

- To Ythaniel's companion she is "a cruel god of a cruel land".
- Lyanir: "They say the vinhili clans eat children in worship of Ilys."

## Earlier notes (2024)

Your 2024 notes: the Ptormic god of moon, stars and sky, twin to Jhaspyr. In earlier versions Jhaspyr and Ilys were twin sister gods. The current draft keeps Ilys female (the Daughter of Moon) and makes Jhaspyr her brother.
