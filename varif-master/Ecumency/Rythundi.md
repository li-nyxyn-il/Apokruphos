# Rythundi

The *Polimunus Rythundi* are the dead gods, "those gods *nogirav*, those gods of ancient might and long lost power, whose dictates were forfeit and designatures forgotten and dominia dust". They are set against the living gods, the Nymuni or Beyonds (see [[Ecumency of the Ten Beyonds]]).

## Rythundi and Nymuni

- Every god, living or dead, has a dominion, a designature and a dictate. In the Lady Rhynise's lesson, Yniel argues the Rythundi are venerated so as not to displease the Nymuni. Sunie argues it is "only because we have always done so".
- "In each Munus lives a mote… Where the mote of the Rythundi is stone, the mote of the Nymuni is a living thing." Each rythundi was "an object of the world elevated by the station of its Munus": *nogirav*, knowledge, a dead thing. The Nymuni's mote is *varigon*.
- "A dead god and a god of the dead are two separate things" (Perythtyrep, on whether Grymsiere is rythundi).
- "[[Jhunus]] molded the nymuni, and he sent them to the sky… From the stars they fell rythundi, and the mountains varigon their tombs."
- The old Shentzlians "worshiped many gods, most now rythundi", above all [[The Phionyx]] and [[Drake]].
- Common soldiers carry pocket idols of the Rythundi to war, bearing "no greater reverence for the gods living than those dead".

## Rythundi Maph (beneath [[Khaska]])

- [[Khunxar]]: The soil
- [[Denma]]: The seasons
- [[Ithkria]]: The rivers and the seas
- [[Xios]]: Law
- [[Yaelde]]: Time, or history
- [[Sysil]]: Courage, judging by the soldiers' prayer (likely Maphlian)
- [[Jhulio]]: Not stated (likely Maphlian)
- [[Earaen]]: Not stated (likely Maphlian)

## Not tied to a Pynumbra

- [[Jhunus]] (Jhun, Jhunz): the creator, a rythundi the Ecumenci assembled from many creation myths.

No rythundi of other Pynumbra are named yet.
