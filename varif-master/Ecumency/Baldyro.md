# Baldyro

Lord of Pleasure, Lord of Pain: the Beyond of the senses and "Nymuni of the Isles of Reim".

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** the [[Reimhurst]] Isles ("the patron god of the Reimhurst Isles").
- **City temple:** [[Bastion]], just south of [[Aldyngrad]]. The stranger in the Penhyla baths has visited "his krypt" on the Holy Path.
- **Atzhaim:** his is one of the two Krypts missing from Atzhaim's seven, along with [[Demios]]'s (see [[Ecumency of the Ten Beyonds]]).

## Dictate

- "The kraiarks of his krypts, in offering to their god, each choose a sense to sacrifice at his altar." One cuts off his ears; another blinds himself and wears his eyes cast in glass about his neck.
- The stranger in the Penhyla baths worships him by indulgence instead: "The whores are his priests! The baths his altar! My wine, his sacrament."

## Designature

- The senses ("Baldyro, whose domain is the senses"). The Lady Rhynise sets him against Khaska: observation against wisdom.
- A constellation, Baldyro, from which embers "cowered".
