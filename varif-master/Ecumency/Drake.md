# Drake

The other Kairal God of old Shentz: Beyond of the mountains, age and death, and the past. His Krypt keeps the law.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** Shentz ([[Shentzlia]]), a Kairal God with [[The Phionyx]]. The *atzh* of the Shentz are "these atzh of the Drake".
- **City temple:** Drazihaim, also called Drazihaim Hylhelm in places, which Hesseh passed through on his way to Maphlamar. Its Krypt of Drake is home to the Hylvannus Kraiark of Law. Your 2024 [[Temple of Drake]] note placed it at [[varif-master/Cities/Great Atzch Road/Cascadia|Cascadia]] in the northwestern Shentzlian valley.
- **Other krypts:** in Atzhaim, his Krypt stands "just beyond the Inner Wall".

## Dictate

- Law. Xerand sends Aiden to ask "the Hylvannus Kraiark of Law" at the Krypt of Drake for Niaviopti's history.
- Marriage dictates are expounded at his Krypt. "The rites dictate that neither party see this until its exposition at the Krypt of Drake."

## Designature

- "A god of the mountains, of age and death and all the crueler things of this world", and the past on the stream of time (Hesseh).
- "Drake is the god of the high, Aiden, but also a God, nogirav. Only the wraiths live there now."
- "That place where Drake walks among dead and living and dead."

## From your planning notes

Drake is called the Judge of Time and is an aspect of the Ayalon fragment; Dane the Drake has something to do with that. Drake/Ayalon is one of the four superclusters.

## Earlier notes (2024)

Your 2024 notes: the Shentzi god of laws and rivers. [[Dane the Drake]] was later deified as Drake.
