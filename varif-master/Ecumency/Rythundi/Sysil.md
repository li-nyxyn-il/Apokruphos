# Sysil

A dead god (rythundi) whose idol Maphyk soldiers carry into battle alongside Xios and Khunxar, so most likely one of the Rythundi of [[Maphlia]], beneath the Nymuni [[Khaska]]. Sunie doesn't name Sysil in her list of the Rythundi Maph.

## Domain

- **Pynumbra:** [[Maphlia]] (inferred from who venerates it)
- **Nymuni:** [[Khaska]]
- **Status:** rythundi, a god *nogirav*. "Their dominia, designatia, and dictates are forfeit in their death" (Sunie).

## Dictate

- Maphyk soldiers carry pocket idols of Sysil into battle.
- War-camp prayer: "A prayer to Sysil for the strength of our courage?"

## Designature

- Courage, judging by the soldiers' prayer.

- Appears in: "Sunie: The Mother’s Voice"
