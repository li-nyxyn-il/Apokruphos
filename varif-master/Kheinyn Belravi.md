# Kheinyn Belravi

## Description

Son of [[Daldos Belravi|Daldoz Belravi]], a blue-blooded warrior of [[Bluestone]]. He trains the blind man with wooden blades and insists there is "a sight beyond sight". The blind man had once trained him. He also rides with [[Vanar Price]] as "the Mountain Lion", in steel and a chain-weapon, testing the loyalty of Vanar's men. The family tree pairs him with [[Ambyr of the Nightblood]] and makes him father of [[Mehania Belravi]].

## From your planning notes

"Kheinyn" is two distinct Belravi golems sharing one name: Demien's trainer at Bluestone and the Mountain Lion. One kills the other at the end of "The Churn". Kheinyn's line "knowledge is a dagger through the mind of man" seeds the book's consciousness cycle.
