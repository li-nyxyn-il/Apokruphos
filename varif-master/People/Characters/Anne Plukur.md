# Anne Plukur

## Description

The Hylvisi Anne of the Line Plukur, wife of [[Ghlenn Jhoryll]] and sister of [[Robert Field]] and [[Mylla Plukur]]. Aiden thinks her more beautiful than any woman he has seen: golden hair, full lips "as red as the dawn's sun" and blue eyes darkening to gray at their edges. She is the only one at breakfast to soften toward him: "He is clearly beyond his station in these matters."
