# Thanyel Field

## Description

Son of the late [[Klaeton Field]] and cousin of the Grymsol Pynumbros [[Robert Field]], married to [[Elyssa Dane]]. He is not a handsome man: a boorish, freckled face, cheeks scarred by childhood warts, a dirty-blond mane of curls and a nose "broken one too many times". At Klate he greets Aiden as "little Dane". At Hyl'viati he arrives with a cocksure smile, wearing the sword Havil gave him as a wedding gift, to deliver the Grymsol's box, and points out Sunie: "There's one!"
