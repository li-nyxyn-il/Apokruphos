# Belise Audsen

> [!NOTE] Name check
> The family tree spells her **Bleise**. The manuscript uses **Belise**.

## Description

Bleyk's daughter, whose mother [[Aylaiah Wren]] died the day after her birth and was buried beneath merrywood blossoms. She looks just like her mother: reddish-blond curls, big blue eyes and a small, freckled nose. When she sees her own blood after a fall, she is horrified that it is red: "Father s--said it was blue!"
