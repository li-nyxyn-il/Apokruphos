# Sunie Dane

## Description

The middle daughter of [[Havil Audsen]] and [[Alynare Dane]], eighteen when the book opens. She has green eyes and soft olive skin, and looks much like her mother, though lighter of skin and fairer of hair. She also has her mother's cunning and a fire that reminds [[Bleyk Audsen]] of their father. She is training in statecraft with the [[Khaska|Khaskylil]], and resents that her parents have never arranged a marriage for her while her little brother [[Aiden Dane]] gets one first.

Sunie adores her half-brother Bleyk and is fond of his children [[Brent Audsen]] and [[Belise Audsen]]. She is cooler toward her older sisters and feels forgotten by her mother. Alynare pulls her into her schemes with a single instruction: "Trust no one, and be trusted by all." When [[Yniel Dane]] is abducted, Sunie rides after her with [[Xerand Leyd]], the "Koloudian fox", only to find that he and [[Ihlven Ihryl]] are the abductors. She escapes alone. Wounded in the mountains, she faces the bear Ursulus, who recites a litany of names: "Ayalon, Ythinyx, Sunie, Sigres, Helyn, Alynare". Later she reaches her father's camp before Hyl'viati, just as [[Thanyel Field]] arrives with [[Robert Field]]'s grim "gift".
