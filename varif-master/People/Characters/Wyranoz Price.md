# Wyranoz Price

## Description

Born 434, died 488. The Price khronologies record him as husband to Sigres Dane (that is, [[Alynare Dane]]) and father of [[Eyhdroz Price]]. Finding his name shows Aiden that his betrothed's great-great-grandmother was a Dane.
