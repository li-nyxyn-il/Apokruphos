# Bleyk Audsen

## Description

Eldest son of [[Havil Audsen]] and half-brother to the Dane children. He is a Hylvaer and a Legionnaire who leads Maphlamar's soldiers, a handsome man with the golden curls his son inherited. He is widowed: his wife [[Aylaiah Wren]], daughter of [[Desmond Wren]], died in childbirth with their daughter [[Belise Audsen]] four years before the story. He dotes on Belise and her older brother [[Brent Audsen]].

Bleyk loathes his stepmother [[Alynare Dane]], the "Dane witch". He believes she has fed on the Audsen Line "like a tick", yet he defends her to [[Sunie Dane]] when he must. He brings home from the Pynumbral Summit the news of [[Aiden Dane]]'s sacrificial union, then rides south against the Koloudian raiders and later commands his own camp in the mountains.
