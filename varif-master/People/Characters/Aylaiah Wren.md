# Aylaiah Wren

## Description

Daughter of [[Desmond Wren]] and wife of [[Bleyk Audsen]]. She died in childbirth with their daughter [[Belise Audsen]] four years before the story and was buried beneath a canopy of merrywood blossoms. Belise has her reddish-blond curls and freckles.
