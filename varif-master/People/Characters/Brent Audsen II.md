# Brenthausen Audsen II

> [!NOTE] Name check
> The manuscript calls him **Brenthausen Audsen II**. Rename this note in Obsidian to match.

## Description

Heir to the Umbral Throne and the station of [[Grymsol Elohaim]], son of [[Brentausen Audsen I|Brenthausen Audsen I]]. His death, "sudden and painless", is announced in the letter Sunie reads in "The Bloodied Man". He left behind his wife and sister [[Cassa Audsen|Kassa Audsen]] and their children [[Krysten Audsen]], [[Kanyn Audsen]] and [[Huber Audsen]].
