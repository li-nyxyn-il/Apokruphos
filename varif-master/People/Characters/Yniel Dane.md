# Yniel Dane

## Description

The youngest of the Dane children, with the family's green eyes. As a child she cried outside Sunie's door after Bleyk's tale of Ursulus, until [[Erynalda Dane]] carried her to bed. She dotes on [[Aiden Dane]] and offers to go with him on the Pylgrymage; he refuses, and later regrets it. She shares a "young flame" with [[Ohsymondi Burch]].

Her mother treats her as a token of trade, to be married into a peace with Ayn'daiun. She is abducted by [[Ihlven Ihryl]] and [[Xerand Leyd]]. [[Sunie Dane]], who rode out with Xerand, finds her bound and gagged at their fire, and has to flee without her.
