# Ihlven Ihryl

## Description

Brother of the late Grymsol Pynumbros [[Ztiodyn Ihryl]] and of [[Maiahna Ihryl]], so uncle to [[Jenifyr Price]] and [[Demien Price]]. He was once heir apparent to the Pynumbral seat of Ptormia, and is now the "disgraced Lord of Ptorm". He has wispy silver hair, a narrow face and blood eyes that "glowed like rubies", and speaks no Maphlian without his eunuch. His wife [[Estyr Khaz]] died in childbirth, and their daughter [[Jehanna Ihryl]] died an infant.

He appears unannounced at [[Alynare Dane]]'s council in Maphlamar and escapes it bloodied. With [[Xerand Leyd]] he abducts [[Yniel Dane]]; Xerand claims "Ihlven knows things" about Alynare "not even I knew". Across the lake he meets Jhyn and Jenifyr with his loyal thaern, and lays out the Kamloran and Myhjodi threat to Ptorm.
