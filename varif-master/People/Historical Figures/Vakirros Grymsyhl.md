# Vakirros Grymsyhl

## Description

Vakirros Grymsol (or Grymsiere), "the Conqueror", who led the armies of Yhlgor north to conquer all the Neck. His words "Thus do I say, I shall be reborn in Mehania" are kept as sacred in the Krypts. In [[Ancient Lore]] he is the son of King Brokuun of the Grymsyhl Dynasty. He conquered [[Diyk]] (renamed [[Atzchia]]), took [[Mehni Dane]] as a prize-wife, and named their daughter [[Mehania Dane]]. The Kraiark teaches that he was not divine himself but imbued with Grymsiere's spirit.
