# Ythinyx

## Description

The last child of [[Mehania Dane]], by a [[Nyxians|Nyxian]] noble, stillborn. Her mother named her Ythinyx, "the dead, come to life", and stole north with her. [[The Phionyx]] brought her back to life with the gift of his cold flame, and she lived on into modern days as [[Alynare Dane]] (see [[Ancient Lore]] and [[Dictionary]]). The bear Ursulus names her among Alynare's names in "The Mother's Voice".
