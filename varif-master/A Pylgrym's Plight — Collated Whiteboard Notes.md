## Language and naming

B4 treats Ecumene speech as a pair of inverse functions. A dictate maps an idea onto the world, and an inquiry maps the world back onto an idea.

### Dictate and Inquiry \[B4\]

- A —*Dictate*→ B. A is the **Domain** and B is the **Designature**.
- f(A) is the *varigon* of the domain. f⁻¹(B) is the *nogirav* of the designature.
- f is a dictate, and f⁻¹ is an inquiry.
- There are only two forms of speech: Dictate and Inquiry. Inquiry reciprocates dictition.
- f(A) = B and f⁻¹(B) = A are grouped as **albla**. The group from f⁻¹(B) = A down to f⁻¹∘f(x) = x is **non**.

*Link:* the dictionary agrees. *Varigon* is the process of an abstract idea becoming reality. *Nogirav*, its reciprocal, is the present becoming history or idea. So every dictate is a varigon, and every inquiry is a nogirav. The draft already has a "court of Wisdom's Dictate," and asks Aiden for "the reciprocal form" of a phrase. **Gnosticism and Truth** (a past chat; your stated points)

- **Ecumene language.** It is a gnostic theology in grammar: every word carries its dialectic in its reverse. Kraiarks fuse a word with its reverse (*yth*, death, + *hty*, life = *ythty*, alive and dead at once). The results are palindromes called **nons**, and some nons are linguistic motes. A major Ecumenci project rebuilds the language from motific nons alone. *Kraiark* is a non, and each kraiark takes a non as a name (Hesseh from Vhinhili *hes*). Aiden will one day realise what word his name reciprocates.
- **Mehaniainahem** ("as above, so below; as below, so above") is intentionally satanic symbolism. Satanism is a perversion of theology, not its inversion. A perversion cannot be synthesised dialectically, and missing that is the Ecumenci's crucial mistake.
- **The Ecumenci and the hive mind.** The Ecumenci are the hive mind's spiritual successor, rebuilding the abstract through the concrete, so *varigon* drives them. The hive mind altered its own memory to believe a splinter sought its destruction. That threat was a deliberate self-deception after it saw its own rot. It dispersed itself to stop understanding itself, reversing the gnostic monad and demiurge. All who rebuild it seed their own doom.
- **Alynare named Aiden on purpose.** "Do not forget your name" ordains him to be in adversity to something. He must grapple with the dialectic, which cannot be unlearned once known. That struggle is also yours, as a non-gnostic "cursed by secret knowledge."
- **Meha nia** is a paradox without reconciliation. Technologically it is the sum of the hive mind's attempts to reverse entropy and escape time, and it stands for regret more than immortality. Alynare embodies it: she seeks to reclaim what was lost and ends with only regret. Brienwyn presses it on Demien, and his arc is to refuse it: reconciliation, not reclamation.
- **Theme: responsibility.** "There are no do overs" for Aiden, Halion, Ythaniel, Jenifyr, Demien and Sunie. Alynare's end is a tragedy and a cautionary tale, likely costing her life. The story is one of redemption, optimistic without denying darkness: good can come from evil without making the evil good.
- **Aiden and Demien** each must choose light they cannot see, out of faith, hope and love alone. In this older plan they meet near the end of Book 2, in the north at Mehania, the last city temple founded by the Yhlgori patriarchs.
