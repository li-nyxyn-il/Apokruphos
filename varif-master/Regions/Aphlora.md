# Aphlora

Aphlora is the six Pynumbri of the [[Umbara]] that share a broad Aphlian culture. When a character says *Aphlora*, *Aphlian*, *Aphlia* or *Aphyk*, they mean this region in general.

## The Aphlian core

- [[Veryll]]
- [[Verdan]]
- [[Hale]]

## The periphery

- [[Kaskaedia]]
- [[Donnia]]
- [[Reimhurst]]

[[Veryll]] also has cultural ties to [[Maphlia]].

## Provinces

- [[Veryll]]: [[Houlyn]], [[Hilos]], [[Ishyn]]
- [[Verdan]]: [[Lorem]], [[Aelvel]]
- [[Hale]]: [[Hylenias]], [[Elohias]], [[Ebyn]]
- [[Kaskaedia]]: provinces not yet named
- [[Donnia]]: [[Myturhem]], [[Donwoud]]
- [[Reimhurst]]: [[Lhokshe]], [[Reim]], [[Sekaentia]]
