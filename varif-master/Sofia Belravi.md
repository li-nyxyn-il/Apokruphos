# Sofia Belravi

## Description

A blue-blooded Belravi, daughter of [[Daldos Belravi|Daldoz Belravi]] and [[Kassia Price]], who serves as [[Emanda Price]]'s handmaid and translator at [[Hylvynhani Falls]]. She is taller than the other women by a head, with sky-blue skin, dark hair and eyes that seem "dark pearls hidden in sapphire", and speaks fluent Shentz. She tutors Aiden in ptormyk and comes to his tent at night. Aiden calls her the most beautiful woman he has ever seen. Her warning that Emanda "will have a brother, in this life or the next" is what first unlocks the secret of the dead firstborns for him.
