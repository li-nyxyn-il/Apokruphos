[[The Phionyx]]: Shentzlian god of creation, fertility, and rebirth. [[Xyioxios]] to the [[Kourzechi]], the blood god of all higher life. To the Aphians the raven mother, to the Maphians, the crow father. The list goes on. This being is mysterious but genial in nature. Phionyx is the source of ancient Shentzlian vampirism, to them known as ‘[[The Cold Flame of The Phionyx]]’. Vampires were seen as holy figures and revered by the early Phionyx cultists. One such Cold Flame was [[Dane the Drake]]. The other Flame Priests deemed him abominable to their order and banished him. He gained power and might in the mountains beyond [[Shentzlia]] and later returned with an army, sailing down the rivers from up in the mountain lands to conquer all of the valley and establish a new order. The Phionyx cult was pushed to the brink of extermination as Dane’s new [Draciian](Dictionary.md) order came to prominence in the Shentzli valley where it ruled for a thousand years. The Phionyx cult fled south into the mountains and wastelands and desert lands beyond the valley. It is here that they began sending out priests to distant corners of the world. One such priest found their way to king named [[The Grymsiere]], who, when the priest told him of the gifts of Phionyx, this cold flame, the [Meha Nia](Dictionary.md) as it was called, what Grymsiere heard was of “The above made below” that would give him eternal life would he follow the priest back home. Thus were the final recorded words of Grymsiere, “I shall be made complete in [[Mehania]].” Which he spoke before following this priest into the northern wastelands beyond the Yhl valley. Grymsiere would soon be betrayed by the priest and offered as a sacrifice to the Phionyx, a truth only known to the ancient priests. For centuries, the Yhlgori believed that Mehania was a place. It was only recent theorizing by Mehanian zealots that Mehania was not just a place but the physical embodiment of the afterlife that had convinced King Brokuun of the Grymsyhl Dynasty that Mehania was in fact a person. Thus began the search for this fabled Mehania that resulted in his son, King Vakirros the Great conquering all of Diyk, which they named Atzchia.  Vakirros, upon meeting the current Shentzli Draci, [[Mehni Dane]], (a name which translated to above all), Vakirros had a vision that the child of Mehni would be the Mehania he sought after. Thus, he claimed Mehni as a prize-wife. His daughter unto Mehni, he named Mehania Grymsyhl.  Truly, however, in ancient Dyikhana tradition, the daughter of a secondary wife was born into the mother’s house. Thus, to all those newly conquered peoples, Mehania was Mehania Dane. Mehania Dane went on to mother nine children as secondary spouse to nine men from across the continent in an effort to unite the realm. These nine men were the first Prelates of the Nine Beyonds.  Mehania’s last child however, a daughter with a Nyxian Noble, (the [[Nyxians]] were the modern, tamer descendants of the Phionyx priests) was stillborn; and in her anguish Mehania swore her child would live and named her [[Ythinyx]], meaning “the dead, come to life”, and stole away with her dead infant to the far north, never to be seen again. Ythinyx would be brought back to life by the Phionyx, giving her the gift of his cold flame. She would live on into modern days, changing her name to [[Alynare Dane]], as whom she would seek to reclaim the ancestral glory of House Dane. 

  Mehania in third epoch Shentzlian is translated to “The flame of the dead earth”.  Thus, in the wake of the Yhlgori invasion of Atzchia, they became convinced that Mehania was beneath a volcano far in the northern reaches of the continent. The Yhlgori elite, their royal guard, and a select few others journeyed north to establish a final city and temple to Mehania where they ventured beneath the earth, leaving behind only the shadow of their former empire in the form of the Royal Ecumency of the Ten Beyonds. 

There is more to the Pheonyx than just this, however. In the lands far east, the Pheonyx is seen not in relation to the red of the flame, but in blue and in black. In the ancient creation myth of the Kourzechi, Xyioxios begot three peoples of the earth. These were the [[Black-Bloods]], the[[ Red-Bloods]], and the [[Blue-Bloods]]. The black-bloods lived in the far east, the blue-bloods in the center of the earth, and the red-bloods to the far west. These three peoples went on to become the lineages of red-blooded humans, the blue-blooded Kourzechi, and the black-blooded Theylix. The Theylix are seen as the youngest of the three lineages, brought into being when Xyioxios took the form of a great, black-feathered bird. It this myth, brought to the lands of Variff by ancient Kourzechi travelers, that became the myths of the Raven Mother and Crow Father among those of the Aphians and Maphians who claim their descent from those ancient blue-blooded peoples. The idea of blue-blood still exists in a morphed form in modern times as a term [Aphloran](Aphlora.md) nobles use to distinguish themselves from the red-blooded common rabble.  This blue-blood is seen as a right to rule bestowed by the [Crow Father](Khodos.md) or the [Raven Mother](Khaska.md), depending upon heritage, either seen to respectively grant higher wisdom and intelligence or health, prowess, and might.
